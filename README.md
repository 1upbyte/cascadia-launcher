# Cascadia Launcher

A minimal, offline Android launcher built around your favorite apps and a
touch-driven alphabet rail. Built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose for Android 10+.
Currently an experimental release.

- Favorites on home, with inline drag reordering and accessible move controls.
- Slide along the alphabet to browse all apps; release to keep browsing.
- Back returns to favorites; Home resets the home screen.
- Clock and date over your system wallpaper.
- Adjustable app size, icon shapes, wallpaper dimming, and alphabet rail placement.
- No accounts, ads, analytics, network permissions, or Google Play services.

Preferences stay on your device. Cloud backup and device transfer are disabled.

Includes [Nunito Sans (SIL OFL)](app/src/main/assets/licenses/NunitoSans-OFL.txt)
and [Octicons (MIT)](app/src/main/assets/licenses/Octicons-MIT.txt).
